Arun

Third Generation Professional Photographer

“A perfect paradise to capture your happiness.”

ARUN'S PHOTOGRAPHY is where the art of photography meets passion, tradition, and timeless storytelling. Rooted in Madurai, Arun continues a family legacy shaped by decades of trust, craftsmanship and dedication to visual excellence.

Today, Arun leads the studio as the third-generation photographer, bringing a fresh, cinematic approach to a tradition that has always been about people and their stories.

With experience across 1000+ weddings, the team specialises in weddings, cultural ceremonies, family celebrations, couple stories and professional visual storytelling — always with authenticity, emotion and elegance.
